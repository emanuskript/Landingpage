This tutorial introduces the principal scripts of the Latin West from Roman antiquity through the early modern period. Each unit surveys a script family or historical phase — its origins, characteristic letter forms, regional variants, and surviving examples. The units are designed to be read in sequence, since later scripts are best understood against the background of earlier ones, but each unit can also stand independently as a reference.
